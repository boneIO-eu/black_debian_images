# Security Policy

The system image built here ships as part of the boneIO Black controller, and
it is covered by the same policy as the application.

**Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.**

Report it through **GitHub private vulnerability reporting** on
<https://github.com/boneIO-eu/app_bbb> (*Security* tab → *Report a
vulnerability*), or by email to <security@boneio.eu> with `SECURITY` in the
subject.

The full policy — what to include, what we commit to in return, disclosure
timelines, safe harbour, scope and the support period — is at
<https://github.com/boneIO-eu/app_bbb/blob/master/SECURITY.md>.

Problems in the provisioning scripts, the default configuration of the image,
its sudo rules, its services and the credentials it ships with are all in
scope, and we want to hear about them.
